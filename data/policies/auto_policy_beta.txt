AUTO POLICY BETA — SYNTHETIC DEMO POLICY
Policy number: AUTO-BETA-001
Effective period: 01 January 2026 to 31 December 2026

COLLISION COVER
We cover accidental collision damage to the insured vehicle when the vehicle is used for ordinary personal transportation, subject to the policy excess and limits.

RACING AND COMPETITIVE USE
Damage occurring while the vehicle is being used for racing, timed competition, track events, or other competitive driving is not covered.

COSMETIC DAMAGE
Minor cosmetic damage that does not affect the vehicle's function may be excluded from collision coverage depending on the applicable repair assessment and policy excess.

GENERAL CONDITIONS
The insured must provide reasonable evidence of the incident and cooperate with the claims investigation.
