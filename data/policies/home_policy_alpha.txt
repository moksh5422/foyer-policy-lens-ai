HOME POLICY ALPHA — SYNTHETIC DEMO POLICY
Policy number: HOME-ALPHA-001
Effective period: 01 January 2026 to 31 December 2026

COVERED PROPERTY
The policy covers the insured residential building and permanently installed household fixtures, subject to the limits, conditions and exclusions below.

ACCIDENTAL WATER DAMAGE
We cover sudden and accidental escape of water from a fixed internal plumbing system, including damage to insured building elements caused by a burst pipe or sudden plumbing failure.

WATER DAMAGE EXCLUSIONS
Damage caused by gradual seepage, repeated leakage, lack of maintenance, or long-term deterioration is not covered.
Damage caused by a pre-existing defect known to the insured before the loss is not covered.

THEFT
We cover theft of permanently installed household appliances when there is evidence of forcible entry, subject to the applicable limit and deductible.

THEFT EXCLUSIONS
Theft of property left in an unsecured external area is not covered under this section.

GENERAL CONDITIONS
The insured must notify the insurer promptly after discovering a loss and take reasonable steps to prevent further damage.
Coverage remains subject to policy limits, deductibles and any applicable exclusions.
